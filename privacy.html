Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Vantage Landscaping LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates vantagelandscapingllc.com. This page explains how we collect and use information when you visit our site or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form, request a quote, or chat with us on the website, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, phone number, address, and project details. We use this to respond to your inquiry and provide you with a quote.

We may also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and IP address through standard web hosting logs.

Chat Widget

Our website includes a live chat feature. Messages you send through the chat are stored to allow us to respond to your inquiry and provide accurate quotes. Conversations may be reviewed by our team for customer service purposes. No chat data is sold to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, schedule services, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain customer information as long as needed to provide our services and as required by law. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

Third-Party Services

We use Jobber (getjobber.com) to manage quotes and customer records. When you submit a work request, your information is stored in Jobber's platform, subject to their privacy policy. We use Netlify (netlify.com) to host this website.

Your Rights
